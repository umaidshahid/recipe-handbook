---
title: Sesame Noodle Bowl
description: Cold noodles, cucumber and a five-ingredient sesame dressing.
tags: [quick, vegetarian]
prep: 10 min
cook: 5 min
serves: 2
---

## Ingredients

- 200 g dried noodles
- 1 cucumber, cut into matchsticks
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds

## Method

1. Cook the noodles according to the packet, then rinse under cold water and drain well.
2. Whisk the tahini, soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil and honey with a splash of water until smooth.
3. Toss the noodles with the dressing, then top with cucumber, spring onions and sesame seeds.

## Notes

Add chilli crisp if you like heat. The dressing keeps a week in the fridge.
