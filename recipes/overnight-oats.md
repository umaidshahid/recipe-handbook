time: 5 min + overnight
serves: 2
---

## Ingredients

- 100 g rolled oats
- 200 ml milk
- 100 g plain yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 pinch salt

## Method

1. Stir the oats, milk, yogurt, chia, maple syrup and salt together in a jar or bowl.
2. Cover and leave in the fridge overnight, or at least 4 hours.
3. Loosen with a splash of milk in the morning and divide between two bowls.
4. Top with one of the combinations in the notes.

## Notes

**Berry:** frozen berries stirred in the night before, plus a spoon of jam. **Apple pie:** grated apple, cinnamon and chopped walnuts. **Peanut:** peanut butter, sliced banana and a little cocoa.
