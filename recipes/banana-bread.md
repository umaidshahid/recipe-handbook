## Ingredients

- 115 g unsalted butter
- 3 very ripe bananas
- 150 g light brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 220 g plain flour
- 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp demerara sugar

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan) and line a 900 g loaf tin.
2. Melt the butter in a small pan and keep cooking, swirling, until it smells nutty and the solids turn golden brown. Let it cool for 5 minutes.
3. Mash the bananas in a large bowl, then whisk in the brown butter, brown sugar, eggs and vanilla.
4. Fold in the flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt until just combined.
5. Scrape into the tin, scatter with the demerara sugar and bake for 55–60 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.

## Notes

Cool in the tin for 15 minutes before turning out. Keeps 3 days wrapped at room temperature.
