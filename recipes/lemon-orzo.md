## Ingredients

- 300 g orzo
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- 900 ml vegetable stock
- 150 g frozen peas
- 1 lemon, zest and juice
- 100 g feta, crumbled
- 1 bunch dill, chopped

## Method

1. Warm the oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the orzo and toast, stirring, for 2–3 minutes until it smells nutty.
2. Stir in the garlic for 30 seconds, then pour in the stock. Simmer for 10–12 minutes, stirring now and then, until most of the liquid is absorbed.
3. Add the peas and cook 2 minutes more. Take off the heat and stir through the lemon zest and juice.
4. Fold in most of the feta and dill. Season, then top with the rest and serve straight away.

## Notes

Keeps for 2 days in the fridge. Loosen with a splash of water when reheating.
