---

## Ingredients

- 1 garlic bulb
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- 1 tsp sugar
- 3 tbsp double cream

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the top off the garlic bulb, drizzle with a little oil, wrap in foil and roast for 25 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, soften the onion in the rest of the oil for 8 minutes.
3. Add the tomatoes, stock and sugar, then simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Squeeze in the roasted garlic, blend until smooth and stir through the cream. Season to taste.

## Notes

Make the grilled cheese while the soup simmers. Freezes well without the cream.
