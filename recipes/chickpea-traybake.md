## Ingredients

- 2 tins chickpeas, drained and patted dry
- 2 red peppers, sliced
- 2 red onions, cut into wedges
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 200 g Greek yogurt
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 garlic clove, grated

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Toss the chickpeas, peppers and onions with the oil, paprika, cumin and a good pinch of salt.
2. Spread out on a large tray in a single layer and roast for 25–30 minutes, shaking halfway, until the chickpeas are crisp.
3. Meanwhile, stir the yogurt with the lemon juice, garlic and a pinch of salt.
4. Spoon the yogurt onto plates and pile the traybake on top.

## Notes

Dry the chickpeas well — that's what makes them crisp. Good with flatbreads or rice.
